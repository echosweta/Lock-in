# LOCK IN — Your Focus. Your Style.

Less scrolling. More shipping.

LOCK IN is your personal focus companion designed to help you stay productive, manage tasks, and build better work habits — all from your Chrome side panel.

### ✨ Features
- **Focus Timer:** Start focused work sessions with customizable durations.
- **Task Manager:** Add, organize, and complete your daily tasks.
- **Anime Themes:** Switch between Default, Your Name, Demon Slayer, Naruto, Attack on Titan, and One Piece themes.
- **Animated Backgrounds:** Bring your favorite anime worlds to life with subtle character animations.
- **Custom Colors:** Personalize your workspace with your favorite accent color.
- **Persistent Side Panel:** Keep your focus workspace open while browsing other tabs.
- **Progress Tracking:** Track completed tasks and focus time.
- **Local Storage:** Your tasks and preferences stay saved on your device.

### 🎨 Make focus your own
Whether you prefer a minimal workspace or an anime-inspired atmosphere, LOCK IN lets you create an environment that makes focusing enjoyable.

Built for developers, students, creators, and anyone trying to get things done.
